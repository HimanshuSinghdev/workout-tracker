Privacy Policy — Train Log

Last updated: September 2026

Train Log is a personal workout-tracking app. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

What we collect

  • If you sign in with Google: your name, email address, and profile photo, as provided by Google Sign-In. This is used only to identify your account so your data can sync across your own devices.
  • Workout entries you log: dates, weights, optional notes, and optional progress photos you choose to add.

How your data is stored

If you use the app without signing in, your entries are stored only on your own device and are never sent anywhere.

If you sign in with Google, your entries and photos are stored in a private Firebase (Google Cloud) database, accessible only to your own signed-in account. No other user can read or write your data.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, share, or use your data for advertising.
  • We don't use your data for any purpose other than running the app.
  • We don't share your data with any third party.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries or photos at any time from within the app. To delete your account's data entirely, contact us using the email below.
